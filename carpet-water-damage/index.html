Wet carpet looks fixable because the surface dries quickly. The trouble sits below it. The pad holds water like a sponge, the tack strips and subfloor stay wet and the whole stack can start to smell within days. If carpet is wet now, call (301) 936-0619. The line is answered 24/7.

Why The Layers Matter

Carpet is a stack of materials. The fibers sit on a backing, the backing on a pad and the pad on a subfloor of plywood or concrete. Water that reaches the surface moves through every layer, and the pad is slow to release it.

That is why a fan alone rarely finishes the job. Extraction pulls out the bulk of the water, and the layers below need directed airflow and dehumidification.

Salvage Or Replace

The deciding factor is the water category. Clean water from a supply line may allow carpet to be lifted, the pad replaced and the carpet dried and reinstalled, if the carpet is not delaminating and the time window has not passed. Water from a flood, a sewer backup or a dirty source means carpet and pad are removed.

Age, quality and how long the carpet stayed wet also matter. Carpet that has been wet for more than a day or two, or that smells musty, is much less likely to be saved.

Before We Arrive: Safety Steps

Wet carpet safety

Turn off power to a wet room if you can do so from a dry spot, and keep electronics off the floor. Do not use a household vacuum on wet carpet, because it is a shock risk. Walk on it as little as possible and place foil or wood blocks under furniture legs to prevent stains.

Take photographs before moving anything. If the water came from a toilet, a drain or the outdoors, keep children and pets off the carpet.

How We Dry Wet Carpet

The crew starts with extraction, using a weighted wand to pull water through the carpet and pad. A moisture meter and a thermal camera then show what stayed wet. If the carpet can be saved, it is peeled back from the tack strip, the pad is removed and discarded and the subfloor is dried.

Air movers and dehumidifiers run until the readings fall, following the ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage restoration standard as a reference. The carpet is dried with air flowing under it, then relaid on new pad.

Insurance And Carpet Claims

Insurers look at the cause, the timing and the condition of the carpet. A sudden leak is often treated differently from long-term seepage. We document the area, the moisture readings and what was removed, and your insurer decides coverage. Keep a piece of removed carpet if the adjuster asks to see it.

Mistakes To Avoid

  • Running a fan on wet carpet and calling it done.
  • Leaving the pad in place because the surface feels dry.
  • Keeping carpet that smells musty after a flood.
  • Putting furniture back before the subfloor reads dry.

Common Questions

Is emergency carpet water damage restoration available in Temple Hills?

The line is staffed around the clock, and a crew can extract wet carpet the same day when one is free. Stay off wet carpet and keep electronics away.

Can wet carpet always be saved?

No. Clean water and quick action help. Contaminated water, long wet times and a musty smell usually mean replacement.

Why is the pad replaced instead of dried?

Pad holds water, is cheap to replace and is slow to dry. Replacing it gives the subfloor a chance to dry properly.

How soon should wet carpet be dried?

The EPA advises drying water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth.
