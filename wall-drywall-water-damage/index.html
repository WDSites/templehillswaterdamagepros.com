What Water Does Inside A Wall

Drywall is gypsum between paper faces. Water breaks down the gypsum and feeds mold on the paper, and the damage often starts at the bottom of the wall and climbs. Insulation holds water against the studs, and the bottom plate and framing can stay wet.

Paint is a poor guide. A wall can look dry while the cavity still reads wet on a moisture meter, which is why repair starts with measurements, not appearances.

Sources Behind The Wall

Supply and drain pipes, window leaks, a roof leak that ran down inside the wall, appliance connections and an AC drain tube can all wet a wall. The wall is often the last place the water shows up. A stain two feet above the floor may mean water entered above it.

For leaks that started at the roof, see roof leak water damage cleanup. For plumbing sources, the plumber's repair comes first and wall repair follows.

First-Hour Safety Steps

Wet wall safety

Shut off the water if a pipe is the source. Do not cut into a wall that may contain wiring, and do not touch outlets or switches on a wet wall. Turn off the breaker for that circuit if the wall is dripping.

If drywall is sagging or bulging, keep clear of it because it can fall. Take photographs and mark the edge of the stain with a pencil so we can see whether it grows.

How We Dry And Repair The Wall

The crew maps the wall with moisture meters and a thermal camera, then removes wet insulation and drywall at the damaged section, usually cutting a straight line above the wet edge. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the framing, and readings are taken until the studs are at normal levels.

Repair follows only when the cavity is dry. New insulation and drywall are installed, taped and finished, and the wall is primed and painted to match. We use the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard as a reference for the drying work, and the EPA advises drying water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth.

Insurance And Wall Damage

Policies often cover sudden water damage and exclude long-term seepage. The timeline of a stain matters, so take dated photographs. We document readings, what was removed and the repair steps, and your insurer decides coverage.

Mistakes To Avoid

  • Painting over a stain before the cavity reads dry.
  • Closing the wall with new drywall to hide the damage.
  • Using bleach on a large moldy area.
  • Repairing the surface without fixing the source.

Common Questions

Is emergency wall and drywall water damage service available in Temple Hills?

Someone takes the call at any hour, and a crew can open and dry a wall the same day when one is free. Shut off the water if a pipe is the source and keep clear of sagging drywall.

Can wet drywall be dried without cutting it out?

Sometimes, if the water was clean and the wall was caught quickly. Soft, crumbling or stained drywall is usually removed.

Why is insulation removed from a wet wall?

It holds water against the framing and dries slowly. Replacing it speeds drying.

Will paint hide a water stain?

Only for a while. Stain blockers help on a dry wall, and the stain returns if the cavity is still wet.
