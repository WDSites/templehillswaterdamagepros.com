Water damage is rarely one job. A leak starts with stopping and drying, moves to cleaning, and ends with a rebuild. We split the work into six services so you can see what each one covers and what it does not. The homepage covers water damage restoration in Temple Hills as a whole, and the pages below go deeper.

Our Six Services

Water mitigation

Emergency water mitigation stops the damage in the first 24 to 72 hours with source control, moisture mapping, extraction and drying equipment.

Water damage cleanup

Water damage cleanup removes wet debris and materials that cannot be saved, then sanitizes and deodorizes.

Water damage repair

Water damage repair rebuilds drywall, insulation, flooring, paint and trim once the structure is dry.

Flood damage restoration

Flood damage restoration handles storm, creek and groundwater flooding, basements and sewage backups.

Emergency water removal

Emergency water removal puts pumps and extraction units on standing water, including burst pipes and appliance failures.

Mold remediation

Mold remediation contains and removes mold after moisture, and addresses the moisture source.
