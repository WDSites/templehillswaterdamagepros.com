Water damage is rarely one job. A leak starts with stopping and drying, moves to cleaning, and ends with a rebuild. We organize the work in two layers. Six core services describe the stage of the job, and fifteen narrower pages describe the cause, the room or the material. The homepage covers water damage restoration in Temple Hills as a whole.

Core Services By Stage Of The Job

Water mitigation

Emergency water mitigation stops the damage in the first 24 to 72 hours with source control, moisture mapping, extraction and drying equipment.

Water damage cleanup

Water damage cleanup removes wet debris and materials that cannot be saved, then sanitizes and deodorizes.

Water damage repair

Water damage repair rebuilds drywall, insulation, flooring, paint and trim once the structure is dry.

Flood damage restoration

Flood damage restoration handles storm, creek and groundwater flooding.

Emergency water removal

Emergency water removal puts pumps and extraction units on standing water.

Mold remediation

Mold remediation contains and removes mold after moisture, and addresses the moisture source.

Find Your Cause

Frozen pipe

Frozen pipe water damage covers a split pipe that leaks after the thaw.

Water heater

Water heater leak cleanup covers a failed tank or fitting.

Appliance

Appliance leak water damage covers washers, dishwashers, fridges and supply lines.

Air conditioner

AC leak water damage repair covers a clogged drain line or overflowing pan.

Slab leak

Slab leak water damage covers a pipe leaking under the foundation.

Sewage

Sewage backup cleanup covers contaminated water from a drain or sewer line.

Find Your Room Or Building

Basement

Crawl space

Commercial

Commercial water damage restoration covers shops, offices and other buildings.

Find Your Material
