I Smell Something Musty, Or See Dark Spots On The Ceiling. What Is It?

A musty odor in a basement, dark spots on a bathroom ceiling, black freckles on a closet wall or a cluster of spots under a sink are all things people notice before they learn the word remediation. They often mean moisture has been present for a while. The question to answer first is where the water is coming from.

Callers describe the same sources again and again. An upstairs toilet leak, an air-conditioner condensate line that backed up, a roof or siding leak, and a bathroom vent that never sent steam outdoors are all common. If the water came from a recent loss, see water mitigation for drying, because dry materials do not grow mold.

Mold Testing And Inspection

Some callers want a test first, and some want a quote first. A visual assessment and moisture readings often tell you what you need to know. Air or surface sampling can add information, but a lab number does not tell you if a home is safe, and it only has meaning compared with outdoor air or a clean reference.

Independent clearance testing, done by a company other than the one that removed the mold, is a separate service that buyers, lenders and landlords sometimes ask for. The remediation company should be willing to tell you what the final check is and how it is documented. Do not pay for testing without asking what the result will be compared with.

Mold And A Home Sale Or Loan

In a sale, a buyer's lender or inspector may require mold to be removed and documented before closing. That puts a deadline on the work. Tell the dispatcher about the deadline, ask for written scope and final-check records, and understand that we cannot promise a lender will accept them. The lender decides what it requires.

Black Mold, Mold On Wood And Mold After Water Damage

People search for black mold because it is the version they have heard about, but color does not identify a species or a risk level. Any mold growth gets the same practical response: fix the moisture, contain, remove damaged material and clean the rest. Do not rely on bleach or a spray to kill mold on porous material, because the roots go into wood and drywall.

Mold on framing or structural wood is cleaned by physical removal, such as HEPA vacuuming and sanding or wire brushing in containment, and the wood is dried. Wood that is soft or rotted is replaced. Mold after water damage is the common case, and it is why water damage cleanup and fast drying matter so much.

Before you disturb mold

Do not scrub or sand moldy material without protection, since that spreads spores through the house. Keep children and anyone who is worried about their health out of the area, turn off fans that would blow spores around, and call. Health questions go to a physician.

Mold Questions In Temple Hills Homes

Homes in Temple Hills built from the 1950s through the 1970s often have basements, older bathroom fans and single-pane windows that sweat in winter. Garden apartments add shared walls, so a leak next door can show up as mold in your closet. If a neighboring unit is the source, tell the property manager and keep photos.

Maryland passed a mold remediation licensing law in 2008, and a 2016 state legislative review found the program had never been funded or put into effect. Rules can change, so ask any company what Maryland requires of it today, and ask for proof of liability insurance. If you live in Oxon Hill or Suitland, the same questions apply.

Mold work is priced by what has to be done, so the price comes after the assessment.

  • Containment. Plastic barriers and air scrubbers add setup time.

Industry Standards And Safety Basics

The industry reference for mold work is the ANSI/IICRC S520 standard for professional mold remediation. It describes assessment, containment, removal and verification as separate steps, which is why a mold job should begin with a clear picture of the moisture source. Keep children, pets and anyone with breathing problems out of the area until it is handled, and do not disturb a large patch with a dry brush or a household fan.

Common Questions

Can you tell me if the mold in my house is dangerous?

We cannot make health claims. We can assess where mold is, why it is there and what removal involves. Questions about symptoms or risk belong with a physician.

Will mold remediation fix the problem for good?

Remediation removes the growth, but mold needs moisture. If the water source is not fixed, mold can return. The assessment looks for the source and the scope explains what has to be done about it.

Do I need testing before removal?

Not always. A visual assessment and moisture readings often show what is happening. Testing can help in some cases, including clearance testing after removal, but it should be requested with a clear purpose.

Does Maryland license mold remediation companies?

A 2008 Maryland law created a licensing requirement, but a 2016 state review found the program had not been put into effect. Rules can change, so ask any company what is required today and ask for proof of liability insurance.

My house is being sold and mold turned up on the inspection. What now?

Get a written scope for removal, a plan for fixing the moisture source and documentation of the final check. We cannot promise what a lender or buyer will accept, so ask them what they require.
