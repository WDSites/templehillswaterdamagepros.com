How Roof Water Travels Inside

Shingles shed water, and the layers beneath them back that up. When flashing, a penetration or a damaged section fails, water gets under the roof covering and onto the deck. From there it runs along the underside of the sheathing, soaks attic insulation and finally reaches the ceiling drywall.

The delay between the leak starting and a stain appearing may be days or weeks. By then insulation can be heavy with water, and framing may be damp.

Safety Steps When A Ceiling Is Dripping

Roof leak safety

Stay out from under a bulging ceiling. Place a bucket under a drip, and puncture a bulge only if a professional says so. Turn off the breaker for a circuit that is wet, and do not use lights or fans in the wet room.

Do not climb onto a wet or icy roof. Wait for a roofer, and call us to deal with water inside.

What Our Crew Does Inside

The crew uses moisture meters and a thermal camera to map the leak path through ceiling, wall and attic. Wet insulation is removed and bagged, and ceiling drywall is cut where it is soaked or sagging.

Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the cavity, following the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard as a reference. The EPA advises drying water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, which is why we start as soon as the leak is under control. Roof Leaks And Insurance

Insurers often cover sudden roof damage from a storm and exclude wear and long-term seepage. A dated photo record helps. We document the interior damage, and your insurer decides coverage. A roofer's report on the cause is useful for your claim.

Mistakes To Avoid

  • Patching the ceiling before the attic is checked.
  • Leaving wet insulation in place.
  • Climbing a wet roof to find the leak.
  • Using a fan to dry a ceiling that is still sagging.
