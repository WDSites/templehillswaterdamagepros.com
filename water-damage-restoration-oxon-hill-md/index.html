Housing That Grew From The 1940s To The Early 1990s

Oxon Hill did not appear all at once. Development began in the 1940s and ran through the early 1990s, and the older end of that range is where aging plumbing shows up. The Eastover shopping center dates to about 1955 and Rivertowne to about 1985, and the homes and apartments around them carry the same range of ages.

A 1950s house may still have its original drain lines, while a 1980s townhouse is more likely to have a failed water heater or a supply hose that has gone brittle.

When Water Is Spreading In Oxon Hill

If water is spreading across a floor or coming through a ceiling, treat it as an emergency, since the damage grows every hour. The steps below limit the loss while dispatch is arranged. They never replace a professional assessment.

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the main water valve. In a condominium, ask the building manager to close the riser.

Power with care

Turn off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel on dry floor. If not, call the utility.

Lift and move

Raise furniture onto blocks, and carry electronics, papers and rugs to a dry room.

Keep out of dirty water

If water is from a sewer or a creek, keep children and pets out and do not wade through it.

Document

Take photos and video before moving anything, so your insurer has a record.

Near The Potomac And Henson Creek

Oxon Hill lies along the Potomac, and Prince George's County says the Potomac is tidal along its entire length in the county. A tidal river and a creek like Henson Creek, which runs past the community, mean low-lying lots can see high water during coastal storms. The county's flood history lists Hurricane Isabel in September 2003, which caused mainly wind damage and power outages with localized flooding.

That history is a reason to look up your own address rather than assume. The county points residents to FEMA's flood maps and to its flood management office for help reading them. Flood coverage is usually a separate policy, so the time to ask is before a storm, not after.

Industry Standards And Safety Basics

Professional water work in Oxon Hill is commonly guided by the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration. It sorts water by contamination category and drying by moisture readings rather than by guesswork. Until a crew arrives, stay out of rooms with sagging ceilings, do not use electrical devices near standing water, and do not mix cleaning products. If you smell gas or see sparks, leave and call 911.

I live in a condominium and the leak is upstairs. What should I do?

Tell the building manager right away so the water can be shut off at the riser, take photos of the damage in your unit, and call us. The building's insurance and yours may both be involved, so keep your records.

Does the Henson Creek area flood?

The county has a flood study on file for the Henson Creek watershed from April 1986, and it states that low spots can flood even away from a stream. Look up your address on the FEMA flood map and ask your insurer about flood coverage.

Will you work with my apartment's property manager?

Yes. We document the loss in each affected unit and provide the property manager with the scope. The manager and the insurers decide who pays.
