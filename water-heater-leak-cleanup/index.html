A water heater leak is one of the most common indoor water losses, and it often goes unseen. The tank sits in a closet, a utility room or a corner of a basement, and a slow drip can run for days. If water is pooling around yours, call (301) 936-0619. The line is answered 24/7.

Where A Water Heater Leak Starts

Not every wet floor around a heater means the tank has failed. Leaks come from the drain valve, the temperature and pressure relief valve, the supply and outlet connections, the drain pan line or the tank itself. A fitting leak can be tightened or replaced, while a leaking tank body means the heater is at the end of its life.

The difference matters for cleanup because a fitting drip is usually small, while a failing tank can release everything in it. A typical residential tank holds many gallons, and that water goes toward the lowest drain, which may be behind a wall or under flooring.

Water Heaters, Permits And Plumbers In Prince George's County

Prince George's County's plumbing permit page says permits are generally not required for replacing existing plumbing fixtures, with the exception of water heaters and testable backflow devices. It also notes that WSSC issues plumbing permits. A replacement heater is therefore not a casual swap.

We handle the water, drying and cleanup. A plumber replaces or repairs the unit, and your plumber can tell you what the permit step looks like for your address.

Safety Before You Touch Anything

Water heater safety

WSSC's winter brochure says electricity must be turned off before draining or refilling an electric tank, and that gas heaters should have the gas valve closed. Do the same before reaching toward a leaking unit. Close the cold water supply valve on top of the tank to stop new water from entering.

Do not stand in water near an electric heater or panel. If you smell gas, leave and call 911 and the gas utility. Hot water from a leaking tank can scald, so keep children and pets out of the room.

How The Cleanup Works

Extraction comes first, using wet vacuums and a portable extractor on flooring. Because heaters often sit on concrete beside finished rooms, the crew checks the wall bottoms and subfloor edges with moisture meters, and pulls baseboard where water has crept behind it.

Air movers and dehumidifiers then dry the closet or utility space, and carpet or pad is lifted when it cannot be saved. Drying is planned with the ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage restoration standard as a reference. Water from a supply-side leak is typically clean at first, but standing water that sits becomes a concern.

Heater leaks range from a damp pad to a soaked room. These factors decide where yours falls.

  • How long it leaked. Days of seepage reach farther than a single burst.
  • Location. A basement slab dries differently from a second-floor closet.
  • Flooring. Carpet, laminate and tile each respond differently to water.
  • Wall damage. Wet drywall behind the heater adds removal and rebuild work.
  • Drying days. Closed utility rooms need airflow and dehumidification.

Insurance And A Failed Tank

A sudden tank failure is usually treated differently from slow seepage that went unnoticed. Many policies cover the resulting water damage but not the heater itself, and the details vary. We document the affected area with photos and readings, keep a record of removed material and leave the coverage decision to your insurer. Renters should send a written notice to the landlord the same day.

Mistakes To Avoid

  • Leaving the heater powered or the gas on while water pools near it.
  • Capping the relief valve pipe to stop a drip.
  • Mopping the floor but leaving carpet pad and baseboard wet.
  • Waiting to find out whether the heater is repairable before drying the room.

Common Questions

Is emergency water heater leak cleanup available in Temple Hills?

Yes. Someone answers at any hour, and a crew is sent for a leaking tank the same day when one can be spared. Close the cold supply valve and switch off power or gas to the heater while you wait.

Do I need a permit to replace a water heater in Prince George's County?

The county's plumbing page says permits are generally not required to replace existing fixtures, but water heaters are an exception, and WSSC issues plumbing permits. Ask your plumber to confirm the steps for your address.

Is water from a leaking heater safe to touch?

The water may be hot, and it may be near electrical connections. Shut off power first, wear boots if you must enter and keep children and pets out until the room is dry.

Does cleanup include replacing the water heater?

No. A plumber replaces or repairs the heater. We extract water, dry the structure and document what was affected.
